# AMBIGUITIES

Each item: what is unclear, how I resolved it, and what it changes. Items marked **(numbers)** move a printed figure.

1. **Replay order vs booked day. (output)** E10 is booked Day 5 but listed after E9 (Day 6). "Replayed in this order" and "per day" can't both be literal. Default: stable-sort by booked day (E10 before E9) so each day's close happens once, in order. `--strict-order` replays the exact list; E10 then arrives after Day 5 closed and is flagged as late-arriving. A test asserts both give identical final balances and fees. E10 touches only ACC-002, so nothing in ACC-001 can differ.
2. **Circular fee test.** "Fee when closing balance is negative" and "the fee is booked on that day" are circular if the fee counts in its own test. Resolved: test the balance *before* that day's fee; fees from earlier days do count in later days.
3. **Retroactive fees. (numbers)** E7 is booked Day 5 but value-dated Day 2. The rule is defined on value-dated balances, so at the Day 5 close the Day 2, 4 and 5 balances are all negative and three fees are assessed, booked Day 5 with value dates D2, D4, D5. Contemporaneous-only assessment (Day 2 had already closed at +250.00) would give one fee at most and would contradict the spec's own criterion 1, which reads Day 2 as -370.00 at end of Day 5.
4. **Fee cascade.** The D2 fee (value D2) lowers D3, D4, D5 too. D3 stays positive (30.00 - 25.00 = 5.00) so no fee. This is close: with any fee above AED 30.00, Day 3 would also go negative and a fourth fee would appear. The cascade is processed in ascending day order.
5. **AED fee on a BHD account.** The spec gives one fee in AED and two accounts in two currencies. No FX rate is supplied, so I raise `LedgerError` rather than invent one. Never triggered here (ACC-002 never goes negative); covered by a test.
6. **Interest basis: restated or contemporaneous? (numbers)** Interest is computed at capitalization from the final value-dated closing balances (including E7+E9 net-zero and the fees). The alternative, freezing each day's accrual at its own close, would book interest on the +250.00 Day 2 balance that E7 later rewrote, then require adjustments the append-only ledger cannot make in place. Different answer: D2 would accrue 0.10 instead of 0.09.
7. **Does Day 6 interest include the capitalization?** No: circular. Day 6 accrues on the pre-capitalization 390.00; the credit posts afterwards, value D6. Fee check for Day 6 also uses pre-capitalization balance.
8. **Interest rounding and "must sum exactly". (numbers)** Daily accruals are rounded amounts at the account's precision, and they must sum to the capitalized total. Summing independently rounded days gives 0.93; the exact total is 0.918 which rounds to 0.92. I use cumulative rounding: today's accrual = round(cumulative exact) - already assigned, so the sum equals round(exact) and the residual is carried, never dropped. Result: 0.10, 0.09, 0.25, 0.17, 0.15, 0.16.
9. **Simple vs compound.** "0.04% per day on the closing ledger balance" with capitalization at the end: accruals do not compound intra-window and do not accrue on each other.
10. **What the interest balance includes.** Fees count (they are ledger entries); holds do not (ledger, not available balance).
11. **Which holds count at authorization.** Available = ledger balance as of the auth's value date, minus *all* currently active holds (any value date), minus the new hold. The spec says "active holds" without dating them.
12. **Auth-B outcome and timing. (output)** At E8 the Day 5 balance is -155.00 and the close's fees (which would only deepen it) have not run yet. -155.00 - 90.00 = -245.00 < 0, so Auth-B is DECLINED. Criterion 5 ("if Auth-B is approved...") is therefore vacuous for this stream; I verify its content on a separate funded account.
13. **Settlement semantics.** Settlement posts a ledger debit for the settled amount, and the *whole* hold is released (not just the settled part). Settling above the hold is rejected. A settlement does not re-test available balance: the hold already reserved those funds at approval time.
14. **Auth-A approval vs later back-dated debit.** Auth-A was properly approved on Day 2 (250.00 - 200.00 = 50.00 >= 0). E7 later makes Day 2 -370.00. I do not re-evaluate or retract the approval (append-only, and decisions are point-in-time). This is the subject of the designed failing test.
15. **Debits may overdraw.** The only gate in the spec is on authorizations. A DEBIT is accepted even into overdraft; the fee rule exists because overdrafts are possible. E7 depends on this.
16. **Reversal mechanics.** A reversal appends a counter-entry with the original's amount negated and the given value date (D2 for E9, equal to E7's). The original is untouched. Only CREDIT/DEBIT can be reversed; a second reversal of the same event is rejected. Reversing settlements would need hold reinstatement, which the spec does not define.
17. **"Three equal instalments" of 10.000 BHD.** Exactly equal is impossible in fils. I use 3.333 / 3.333 / 3.334 (residual on the last). All three are value-dated Day 5 as the spec states. A reversal of E10 would reverse all three.
18. **E6 / Auth-Z.** "No preceding authorization event" and "not present in the ledger" are treated as the same thing. The event is REJECTED (an error, not an authorization decline): no ledger entry, no money moves. Auth-Z appears in state tables as UNKNOWN so the rejection stays visible.
19. **Closing balance with fees.** "Closing ledger balance" is shown both pre-fee and post-fee, because criterion 1 explicitly needs pre-fee and the fee rule defines post-fee as the number that persists.
20. **E9 and fees.** E9 reverses E7 only. Three fees were already assessed on balances that E9 later makes non-negative. No rule says to refund them, and inventing a refund would add a ledger entry the spec never asked for, so the fees stand.
21. **Opening balances.** ACC-001 0.00 and ACC-002 0.000 are modelled as no entries (balance sums to zero), not as synthetic opening entries.
22. **Intra-day ordering.** Only booked-day granularity is specified. Within a day, order is the stream order (the sequence counter). Authorizations on a day see only what was booked before them in the stream.
23. **Interest on ACC-002.** 10.000 BHD x 0.04% = 0.004 BHD exactly per day on D5 and D6, so 0.008 BHD, an exact multiple of the BHD unit; no rounding needed. D1-D4 balances are zero: no accrual.
24. **Value date for capitalization.** Day 6, booked Day 6, posted after fee assessment.
25. **Input precision.** An amount with more decimals than its currency allows is an error, not silently rounded: "amounts stored and rounded to their own precision" is read as "stored at that precision", and rounding is only applied by the engine itself (interest).
26. **Sequence numbering.** One counter is shared by journal entries and decision records so the two logs can be merged into a single total order; consequently `#seq` values on journal entries have gaps.
